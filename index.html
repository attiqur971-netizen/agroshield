Help & FAQ

Find answers to common questions about AgroShield and learn how to use the platform effectively.

How does the AI Crop Scanner work?
The AI Crop Scanner analyzes leaf images using computer vision to detect disease patterns. You can use your webcam for live scanning, upload an image, or select a preloaded sample. The system matches visual symptoms against our disease database and provides a full diagnosis report.
How do I purchase medicines from the Remedy Shop?
Click "Buy Sample" on any product in the Remedy Shop. Our team will contact you via WhatsApp to confirm the order and arrange delivery. You can also call us directly at 03196352084.
Is my scan history saved permanently?
Your scan history is saved locally in your browser. It stays as long as you use the same browser and device. Clearing browser data or switching devices will reset the history.
Can I use AgroShield on mobile?
Yes! AgroShield is fully mobile-responsive. On mobile you can use your rear camera to scan crop leaves directly from the field. Tap "Start Webcam" in the Scanner section and grant camera permissions.
How accurate is the disease diagnosis?
AgroShield's AI is trained on thousands of crop disease images from Pakistani agricultural regions. For best accuracy, ensure the leaf is well-lit and the affected area is clearly visible. For critical decisions, always consult our specialist Dr. Hakeem Hamid Raza.
What crops does AgroShield support?
Currently AgroShield supports Tomato, Corn (Maize), Wheat, Potato, Rice, and Cotton. We are expanding to include Sugarcane, Sunflower, and Mango soon.
How do I report a bug or technical issue?
Go to Contact Us and select "App Bug / Technical Issue" from the dropdown. Describe the issue in detail. Our dev team reviews all bug reports within 48 hours.

Still have questions? Our team is ready to help.
